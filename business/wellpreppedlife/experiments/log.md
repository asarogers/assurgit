## EXP-001 — Universal opener vs condition-specific
**Status:** ✅ Confirmed
**Opened:** 2026-03-06
**Hypothesis:** Universal opener (not naming a condition) gets 3x+ more reach than condition-specific opener
**Test:** Natural experiment Cards 1–10: Cards 1 and 7 opened with condition names; Card 5 opened with universal empowerment hook
**Success criterion:** 3x improvement on at least one platform
**Result:** Card 5 (universal): 301 YouTube views, 130 Instagram reach. Card 1 (dementia): 3 views, 11 reach. Card 7 (diabetes): 34 views. Differential: 4.5x–100x.
**Notes:** Confirmed at 4.5–100x differential. Universal opener is now the default for all new cards.
